## `> who_am_i`

I design and ship full-stack web products end to end — from the database schema to the deploy pipeline, no handoff in the middle. My focus is **TypeScript on Next.js and Node**, with AI designed into the core of the product rather than bolted onto the end.

I care about the parts most demos skip: the schema, the loading states, the edge cases.

- 📍 **Based in** Bengaluru, India
